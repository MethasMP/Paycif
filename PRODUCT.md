# Product

## Register
product

## Users
Consumers making quick QR payments on the go. They need an interface that is fast, highly legible, and reassuring during financial transactions.

## Product Purpose
A high-performance, security-first fintech application built with a hybrid architecture (Flutter, Go, Rust). It focuses on speed, reliability, and cryptographic security for modern digital payments and transfers. Success looks like frictionless, instantaneous, and secure transactions.

## Brand Personality
Traditional, trustworthy, and institutional. It should evoke confidence and stability.

## Anti-references
- Dark themes that feel too aggressive or overly technical.
- Generic, cluttered banking interfaces.
- Playful or overly casual aesthetics that undermine institutional trust.

## Design Principles
- **Institutional Confidence**: Visuals must convey security and permanence, not experimentation.
- **Velocity of Transaction**: The primary action (QR scanning/payment) must be accessible with minimal friction.
- **Clarity over Cleverness**: Information architecture should be straightforward; no hiding important financial data behind clever interactions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
High contrast for outdoor visibility (on-the-go context). Large, clear tap targets to prevent erroneous inputs during quick transactions. Support for dynamic type scaling.
